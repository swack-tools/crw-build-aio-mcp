# Contributing

Thanks for helping out. Before your first pull request can be merged, you
need to sign the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). The CLA bot will
comment on your pull request with the sentence to post; signing once covers
all your future contributions.

The CLA lets the maintainer accept your change and offer the project under
other terms later (for example, dual licensing). You keep your copyright,
and your change will always stay available under the
[GNU AGPL v3 or later](LICENSE).
